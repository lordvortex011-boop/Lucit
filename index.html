FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to edit anything?

No. Captions, reframe, cuts, titles, hashtags, and scheduling are handled automatically. You can review if you want, but posting does not wait for you.

What do I upload?

One long video per week. Talking head, screen share, or voiceover, 10 minutes to 2 hours, whatever your channel is about.

Where does it post?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. One toggle per platform. Posts are scheduled for watch-time windows instead of all at once.

What if I do not like a clip?

Skip it with one tap. The rest still posts. No re-editing or re-exporting needed.

Is this for beginners chasing trends?

No. It is for solo faceless creators who post consistently and are working toward monetization.
